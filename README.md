## 🎯 Business Problem

A ride-hailing operations team needs a centralized view to answer key
business questions:

-   How many rides are completed or lost?
-   Which vehicle types and payment methods generate the most revenue?
-   How do riders behave across first-time, returning, and regular
    segments?
-   When and where is ride demand highest?

This dashboard brings these metrics together to support operational
analysis and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

1.  Track booking performance (completed vs. lost bookings) and revenue
    over time.
2.  Compare vehicle types by customer count, bookings, and revenue.
3.  Understand revenue by payment method and customer.
4.  Segment riders into first-time, returning, and regular riders, and
    review cancellation reasons.
5.  Identify peak demand time slots and the busiest pickup/drop-off
    locations.

## 💡 Key Insights

1.  **Revenue is relatively steady, with a notable dip in February.**
    Monthly revenue ranges from approximately 4.2M to 4.6M in most
    months, with March peaking at 4.57M. February is the lowest month at
    3.97M.
2.  **Auto leads in revenue and completed bookings.** Auto generates
    12.88M (about 25% of total revenue) from 23,128 completed bookings,
    followed by Bike (11.46M) and Go Mini (10.34M). Uber XL is the
    smallest revenue contributor at 1.53M (about 3%).
3.  **UPI is the leading payment channel.** UPI generates approximately
    23M in revenue (around 44% of the total), ahead of Cash (13M), Uber
    Wallet (6M), Credit Card (5M), and Debit Card (4M).
4.  **First-time riders make up the largest displayed rider segment.**
    The Rider page shows 48K first-time riders, compared with 6K return
    riders and 11K regular riders (three or more rides).
5.  **Evening demand is strongest in the Uber XL view.** The 6 PM--9 PM
    time slot is busiest across the week, reaching its highest displayed
    value on Tuesday (154), followed by 3 PM--6 PM. The 3 AM--6 AM slot
    is the quietest.
6.  **Some cancellation reasons point to service-related issues.** In
    the customer view, "Change of plans" (2,353) and "AC is not working"
    (1,155) are among the listed reasons.
